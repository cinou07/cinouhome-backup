Overview

The short version

Cinou Studios ("Cinou AI", "we", "us") builds a conversational assistant. This policy covers the website at cinouhome.pages.dev and the web app at cinouai.onrender.com. We collect the minimum needed to run the service, we don't sell your personal data, and we tell you here which third parties we rely on and why.

Data we collect

What information do we collect?

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google to identify your account and attribute the comments you post.
  • Conversation content. Messages you send to Cinou AI are processed to generate responses and, where the product feature requires it, kept so a conversation can continue where you left off.
  • Comments. Anything you post in the comments section is stored along with your name and avatar from Google sign-in.
  • Usage and device data. Basic technical information such as browser type, general region, and pages visited, collected automatically to keep the service running and secure.
  • Local storage. Some preferences and demo data (like comments in the browser-only demo mode) are stored locally in your browser rather than on our servers.

Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences. Google Sign-In may itself set cookies as part of authenticating your account; that use is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Third parties

Third-party services we use

  • Google Sign-In. Used for authentication. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Render. Our web app (cinouai.onrender.com) is hosted on Render's infrastructure for a temporarily time.
  • Cloudflare Pages. our home page is hosted on Cloudflare Pages also for a temporarily time, which processes standard request data to serve the site.

We don't sell your personal information to third parties, and we don't share it for third-party advertising purposes.

Retention

How long we keep your data

We keep account and conversation data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time using the contact details below.

Your rights

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we'll respond as required by applicable law.

Children

Children's privacy

Cinou AI is not directed at children under 3 (or the age required in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

How we protect your data

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
