About Cinou AI

One assistant. Every conversation, remembered.

Cinou AI is a smart, easy-to-use AI assistant you can chat with to write, plan, research and create — on the web today, and soon on your phone and PC.

What it is

What is Cinou AI?

Cinou AI is a conversational assistant you can turn to for everyday thinking work: drafting messages, researching a topic, planning a project, brainstorming ideas, or just talking through a problem. It's designed to keep track of the whole conversation — not just your last message — so you don't have to repeat context every time you come back to it.

It's free to start, works right in your browser with no install, and is built to follow you across devices as new apps roll out.

What it does

What features does Cinou AI provide?

  • Context that sticks. Cinou keeps track of an entire conversation, so follow-up questions and long threads stay coherent.
